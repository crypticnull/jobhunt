# 0001: Record decisions as ADRs

Date: 2026-09-01
Status: accepted

## Context

The brief requires sign-off on frameworks, dependencies and spend, and this
repo doubles as a public case study. Decisions explained after the fact read
as reconstruction. Decisions recorded the day they are made read as judgment.

## Decision

Every signed-off choice gets a numbered file in docs/decisions the day it is
made: context, the call, the tradeoff accepted, 25 lines maximum. Commits
that execute a decision cite its number. The plan's decision table was
signed off in full on 2026-09-01; the choices Foundation touches are
recorded today as 0002 through 0007, the rest become ADRs when first
executed.

## Tradeoff accepted

A little ceremony per decision, capped small enough to survive a full-time
job.
