Decks arrive from stakeholders with the only copy of a logo or a photo buried inside them, and pulling assets out by hand is slow and lossy. I wrote a tool that extracts every asset from a Keynote file in one pass, then shared it with the team along with a tutorial, because nobody asked and everybody needed it.
