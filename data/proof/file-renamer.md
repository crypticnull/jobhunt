Deliveries come back with a hundred files that need the same three changes to their names. File Renamer stacks rules, find and replace, prefix and suffix, delimiter swaps, shows exactly what every new name will be before anything is touched, then writes beside the originals or over them. It's pure Python with nothing to install, packages to a single executable, and builds itself on every push. Metadata presets and Iconik integration are the next step, because renaming is the front door to asset management.
