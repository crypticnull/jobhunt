The game is where the whole intersection shows up in one place. I art directed it, designed its systems and wrote it, roughly 33,000 lines of TypeScript with no engine, no framework and no build step. Underneath is Canvas 2D with a hand-written WebGL sprite batcher and a clean fallback when WebGL is missing, running in a browser and as a packaged desktop app from one codebase. Twenty five playable builds shipped in eleven days. Twenty one tools were built alongside it, including a balance system where every tunable number is driven by a runtime slider and then baked back into source, so a balance change arrives as a reviewable diff instead of folklore. The art came out of a 219-node ComfyUI graph that solves multi-angle consistency structurally rather than by prompting, one reference character re-rendered from eight viewpoints, then a scripted correction chain that lands the facings on a common ground line. Most people who can build a pipeline can't judge the frame it makes, and most people who can judge the frame can't build the pipeline. This is the proof that I do both.
