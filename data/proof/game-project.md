The game is where the whole intersection shows up in one place. I art directed it, designed its systems, and generated its custom art through a ComfyUI to Photoshop pipeline I built for the purpose, so the visual language and the tooling that produces it are the same project. Most people who can build a pipeline can't judge the frame it makes, and most people who can judge the frame can't build the pipeline. This is the proof that I do both.
