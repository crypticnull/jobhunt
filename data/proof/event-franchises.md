The through-line in the motion work is ownership of a visual language over time. Quest, Nitro Create, Summit and PAG are recurring flagship events, and I've carried their brand systems across multiple cycles, extending the identity each year instead of starting over. That's key art, animation packages and logo loops that have to feel like the same family a year later while still feeling new, delivered on event schedules that don't move. The turnaround numbers are in the individual project records where they're worth stating.
