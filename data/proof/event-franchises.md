The through-line in the motion work is being handed the same flagship events year after year, and building the identity from the ground up every time. Quest, Nitro Create, Summit and PAG all come back annually, and each cycle gets fresh design and new logo loop work rather than a reskin of the year before. The only fixed points are the Nitro Create and PAG logos, and everything built around them is new. That's key art, animation packages and logo loops that have to carry an event on a stage, in a feed and in a deck, delivered on schedules that don't move. Doing that once is a brand project, doing it every year for four events is why the work keeps coming back to me. The turnaround numbers are in the individual project records where they're worth stating.
